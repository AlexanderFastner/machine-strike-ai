## Skills

All fifteen skills are specified. They collapse into a handful of mechanisms rather than fifteen special cases: four *attack-from-terrain* bonuses (one per terrain tier from Grassland up), three *terrain conversions* on the target's tile, four *start-of-turn auras*, two defensive/reactive effects, plus `Sweep` and `Alter Terrain`.

| Skill | Pieces | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Alter Terrain | Bilegut, Rockbreaker, Slitherfang, Tracker Burrower | After inflicting damage: the terrain under **your** machine lowers a step, the terrain under the **target** rises a step. No terrain gate. |
| Sweep | Ravager, Stormbird, Thunderjaw, Tremortusk | Hits the target **and any machines perpendicular to it**. The exact area is per-piece: Ravager 1×3 two tiles ahead; Thunderjaw and Tremortusk 1×3 directly in front; Stormbird 3×3 in front. |
| Burn | Elemental Clawstrider, Fireclaw, Scorcher | Attacking a machine on **Forest** turns that tile to Grassland. |
| Spray | Bellowback, Bristleback, Slaughterspine | Start of turn: all pieces within Attack Range lose 1 Health. |
| Empower | Leaplasher, Longleg | Start of turn: friendly machines within Attack Range gain +1 Attack Power. Stacks. |
| Gallop | Charger, Grazer | Attacking **from Grassland**: +1 Combat Power. |
| Retaliate | Apex Clawstrider, Rollerback | On being attacked: rotates to face its attacker and deals 1 damage, if the attacker is in range. |
| Shield | Behemoth, Shell-Walker | +1 Combat Power when **defending**. |
| Stalk | Clamberjaw, Stalker | Attacking **from Forest**: +1 Combat Power. |
| Whiplash | Dreadwing, Waterwing | Start of turn: all pieces within Attack Range rotate 180°. |
| Blind | Redeye Watcher | Enemy machines within Attack Range lose 1 Attack Power for the rest of the turn. Stacks. |
| Climb | Lancehorn | Attacking **from Hill**: +1 Combat Power. |
| Freeze | Frostclaw | Attacking a machine on **Marsh** turns that tile to Grassland. |
| Growth | Plowhorn | Attacking a machine on **Grassland** turns that tile to Forest. |
